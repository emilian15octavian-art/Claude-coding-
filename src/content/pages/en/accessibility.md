## Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, keyboard navigation, zoom or high contrast.

## What we do

- The site aims to meet **WCAG 2.2 level AA**.
- Every page is tested automatically on every change (axe-core) in English, Arabic (right-to-left), Russian and Greek, at phone and desktop width, in dark and light mode.
- Photos have text descriptions, videos and players load only when you press play, and all buttons are at least 44×44 pixels.
- The site works without cookies and respects your system settings for dark mode and reduced motion.

## Known limits

- Videos and audio come from YouTube and SoundCloud, whose players we do not control.
- Translations other than English and Romanian still need a native-speaker review.

## Feedback

If something does not work for you, tell us through the [contact form](/en/contact/) or at bleedingdebts316@gmail.com. We reply within 10 working days.
