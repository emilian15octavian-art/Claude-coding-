## The short version

This website does **not** use advertising or tracking cookies, so you will not see a cookie banner. Our visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) work without cookies.

## What is stored on your device

| Name | Where | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Your browser (local storage) | Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode | Until you clear it |
| `langNoticeDismissed` | Your browser (local storage) | Remembers that you closed the language suggestion | Until you clear it |
| `domChat` | Your browser (session storage) | Keeps your conversation with Mr. D.O.M. while you move between pages | Until you close the tab |
| `bd316-…` | Your browser (cache storage) | Copies of pages you already opened, so they still load without internet | Until the next site update or until you clear it |

These entries are only created when you use those features, never leave your device and are not cookies. They are strictly necessary to provide a feature you asked for. Nothing in them is ever sent to us or anyone else.

## Third-party services

- **Videos and music players** (YouTube, SoundCloud) load only when you press play. Those services may then set their own cookies, under their own policies.
- **Checkout**: when you press Buy you go to Stripe's secure payment page, which uses its own cookies for fraud prevention.
- **Contact form**: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are human. It is designed to work without tracking cookies.

## If this changes

If we ever add a service that needs cookies (for example Google Analytics), it will only load after you agree in a consent banner, where rejecting is as easy as accepting.
