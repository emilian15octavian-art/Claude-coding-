## Shipping

- We ship within Romania and to the countries listed at checkout.
- Orders are packed within 1–2 working days.
- Current shipping costs and delivery estimates are shown on the shop page and at checkout.
- You receive an e-mail with tracking details when your parcel leaves us.

## Your 14-day right of withdrawal

You can withdraw from your purchase within **14 days** of the day you (or someone you name) receive the goods, without giving any reason.

1. **Tell us** by e-mail (see the contact page) that you want to withdraw. You can use the model form below, but you do not have to.
2. **Send the items back** within 14 days of telling us. You pay the cost of returning them, unless we agreed otherwise.
3. **Refund**: we refund all payments received from you, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal notice. We may wait until we receive the goods back (or proof that you sent them). We refund with the same payment method you used.

You are responsible only for any loss in value caused by handling the goods more than needed to check them.

**Exceptions:** the right of withdrawal does not apply to items made to your specifications (for example personalised items) or to sealed audio recordings (CD, vinyl, cassette) that you have unsealed after delivery.

## Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or wrong, write to us within a reasonable time with a photo. We will replace it or refund you, including return costs.
