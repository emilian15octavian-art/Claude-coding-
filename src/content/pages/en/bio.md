Some lessons arrive as wisdom. Others leave scars before they make sense.

This is where I turn those lessons into music. Biblical echoes. Hard-earned truths. Heavy music for questions that don't come with easy answers.

I write about the weight of our choices, the debts we carry, and the search for grace in the middle of it all. Faith and doubt. Regret and redemption. The things we bury and the truths that refuse to stay buried.

Not preaching from a place of perfection. Not pretending every wound has a purpose or every struggle ends with certainty. Just honest lyrics from somewhere between what I believe, what I question, and what I'm still learning to face.

For anyone who has ever found a piece of themselves in a verse or needed a song to say what they couldn't.

This is Bleeding Debts 316. The scars don't get the final word.
