## Who are Bleeding Debts 316?

Original metal from Romania, formed in summer 2026: Rush The Flow (RUSH) on production, mixing and composing, and Mr. D.I.P. (D'8ONE) on vocals, lyrics and performance. More on the [About](/en/about/) page.

## Where can I listen to the music?

On the [Music](/en/music/) page and on SoundCloud. The official videos are on the [Videos](/en/videos/) page and on YouTube.

## Are you on Spotify?

No, and that is on purpose. Mr. D.O.M. searched every banana crate in the building: we are not there. SoundCloud and YouTube it is.

## When is the next show?

No shows are announced yet. They appear on the [Shows](/en/shows/) page as soon as they are confirmed, and newsletter subscribers hear first.

## How do I book the band?

Use the [contact form](/en/contact/) or write to bleedingdebts316@gmail.com. Promoters and journalists find bios, photos and the logo pack in the [press kit](/en/press/).

## Is there merch?

The [shop](/en/shop/) opens soon. Join the [newsletter](/en/newsletter/) to be first in line.

## How do I get news about new songs and shows?

Join the [newsletter](/en/newsletter/): a few e-mails a year, never spam, unsubscribe any time. You can also follow every profile from the [links](/en/links/) page.

## Who is Mr. D.O.M.?

The band's monkey assistant 🐒. Tap the button in the corner of any page to ask him about the songs, videos, shows or booking. He is an AI and can make mistakes; for anything important use the contact form.

## Can I use your music in my video or stream?

Ask us first through the [contact form](/en/contact/), with a link to your channel and what you plan to make.
