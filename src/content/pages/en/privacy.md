This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use this website, why, and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

## Who is responsible

The data controller is the legal entity shown in the box above (name, registration number and address). You can reach us through the contact page or by writing to the e-mail address shown there.

## What we process and why

**Visiting the website.** The site is hosted by Cloudflare. When you open a page, Cloudflare processes technical data (IP address, browser type, requested page, time) to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Visitor statistics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits **without cookies** and without building profiles of individual visitors. We only see totals such as page views and countries.

**Contact form.** When you send a message we process your name, e-mail address and message to answer you. The form is delivered through Web3Forms and checked for spam with Cloudflare Turnstile. Legal basis: steps at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) or our legitimate interest in answering messages (Art. 6(1)(f)). We delete messages after 24 months unless we need them longer for an ongoing booking or order.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we store your e-mail address, the language you chose and the time and proof of your consent. You confirm your subscription by clicking a link we e-mail you (double opt-in). The newsletter is sent with Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores data in the EU. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every e-mail.

*Current set-up:* until we start using Brevo, each sign-up (your e-mail address, language and consent) is delivered by Web3Forms to the band's mailbox (Gmail, Google) and kept there only for the newsletter. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

**Shop orders.** Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details directly; we never see them. To deliver your order we receive your name, delivery address, e-mail address and order details. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)), for example keeping accounting records for the period required by Romanian law (Law no. 82/1991).

**Videos and music players.** YouTube, Spotify and Bandcamp players load **only after you click** on them. Until then no data is sent to those services. After you click, their own privacy policies apply.

## Who receives data

We use these processors, each bound by a data processing agreement: Cloudflare (hosting, statistics, spam protection), Web3Forms (contact form delivery), Brevo (newsletter), Stripe (payments) and our courier for deliveries. We never sell your data.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, the United States. Transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To use these rights, contact us. You may also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in your own EU country.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

## Changes

We will update this page if the way we handle data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
