# Bleeding Debts 316: daily growth checklist

Three posts a day, 4.5 hours apart (Bucharest time). The posts are scheduled in Metricool in advance;
this checklist is the part that only you can do, plus what to check so the next week gets better.

## The daily rhythm

| Time | Slot | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12:30 | **Series** | *Road to 10 December*, one episode a day, your own song | A recurring series gives people a reason to follow |
| 17:00 | **Trend** | Photo Reel with a popular Instagram sound on top | Popular sounds get shown to people who don't follow you yet |
| 21:30 | **Prime** | Confessional hook Reel, your own song | Your best format (lowest skip rate) in your best hours |

## Every day (about 25 minutes)

**Before the 12:30 post (10 min)**
- [ ] Engagement round: comment on **10 accounts**, then stop (from your site's `ig-plan` skill)
  - 5 bigger metal or Christian-metal accounts: comment early, in the first hour of their post
  - 3 bands your size: the group that comments back
  - 2 fans or possible fans: real comments, never a pitch
- [ ] Post **one Story**: a behind-the-scenes photo, a poll, or a question box. Stories keep followers seeing you

**Within 1 hour of each post (5 min each)**
- [ ] Reply to **every** comment with a question back, not just a heart. Replies in the first hour push the post further
- [ ] Share the Reel to your Story with a sticker ("new", a poll, or "link")

**Once a day (5 min)**
- [ ] Post **one face Reel** from your phone (10 to 20 s, you talking or playing). Your face Reels get **1,600 to 2,500 views**
      against about 150 for generated ones. This is still the single biggest lever, and it doesn't count toward any posting limit
- [ ] Check DMs and answer them

## Every Sunday (15 min): improve next week

- [ ] Open Metricool analytics: find the **top 3 Reels by reach** and the **3 with the lowest skip rate**
- [ ] Copy what worked: same hook type, same slot, same sound style. Drop the slot type that lost twice in a row
- [ ] Swap the Trend slot's sound for whatever is trending that week (Instagram app: Reels → audio with ↗ arrow)
- [ ] Tell me the winners and I'll make next week's content the same way

## Trends for bands right now (what to lean into)

- **Hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds**, readable with the sound off. Skip rate decides reach more than posting time
- **Trending audio** for discovery posts; **your own song** for posts meant to turn viewers into listeners
- **Series and countdowns** ("Day 12 of…", "10 days until…") bring people back
- **Comment prompts with one-word answers** (a number, an emoji, "amen or headbang") get the most replies
- **Faces and real people** beat graphics on every platform
- **Cross-post every Reel** to Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts: same video, extra free reach
- **Saves and shares** count more than likes: "save this" and "send this to…" prompts work

## Rules

- English only, for now
- No fake engagement: no bought followers, likes or comments
- No Spotify links; the link is your Linktree / the site
- Never post the same video twice on the same account
