## 2. Your face, once a day (biggest lever: your own videos got 760 – 2,546 views)
Phone, vertical, 10–20 s, one take. Say the hook in the first second while looking at the camera. Post it yourself, any time not within 1 hour of a scheduled Reel.

| Day | Say this first (the hook) | Then | End on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Nobody tells you this about screaming..." | show one scream, then say "no pain, it's the false folds and breath" | "Want a 30-second lesson? Comment SCREAM." |
| 2 | "Why I make metal and go to church." | 2 honest sentences, no preaching | "Weird combo or makes sense?" |
| 3 | "This riff took [real number] tries." | play or show the riff on the speaker | "Rate it 1 to 10." |
| 4 | "My day job doesn't know I do this." | quick cut: work clothes, then the mic | "What does yours not know?" |
| 5 | "Reacting to the first song I ever wrote." | 3 seconds of the old song, then your face | "Be honest: better or worse now?" |
| 6 | "Why our band has 316 in the name." | the real story behind the name, in 2 lines | "Did you guess it?" |
| 7 | "10 days until something we've been building in secret." | show the site on your phone, no URL read out | "Want early access? Comment IN." |

Each reply to a comment counts as a second chance at reach. **Answer every comment within the first hour** with a question back.

## 3. Collabs: borrow other audiences (Instagram Collab posts appear on both profiles)
Instagram's Collab feature puts one Reel on both accounts' feeds. It works best with bands your size whose fans are *not* the same as yours.
- **Who:** 10 Christian metal / metalcore / nu-metal bands with 300 – 5,000 followers, plus 5 Romanian rock or metal bands of any faith.
- **What:** a "duet riff" Reel (they play a riff, you answer it), "rate each other's songs", or a split cover. You add them as collaborator before posting.
- **DM to send** (personalise the first line every time, never copy-paste the whole thing):
  > Hey [name], [one real line about a song of theirs you actually listened to]. We're Bleeding Debts 316, a Christian metal duo from Romania. Want to do a Collab Reel? Idea: you post a riff, we answer it, both on both profiles. We'll edit it. Zero pressure if not.
- Aim: 5 DMs a day, 1–2 collabs a week. Track who answered in a note.
